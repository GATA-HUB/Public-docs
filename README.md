# GATA HUB

At GATA HUB, we have been building at the intersection of NFTs, NFTfi, and decentralized infrastructure since the early days of the Cosmos ecosystem.

Our work spans validator operations, IBC relayers, and public infrastructure that helps connect and support decentralized networks. We focus on reliable, practical infrastructure while contributing to the broader ecosystem through public goods and open services.

We also believe NFTs can offer more than collectibility. Through our NFTfi and yield-focused initiatives, we have explored ways for NFT ownership to create real utility and ongoing value for holders.

Beyond infrastructure and NFTs, GATA has built and supported a range of decentralized applications and community-driven initiatives, each shaped by the needs of the ecosystem around us.

Our approach is simple: build useful things, keep experimenting, and contribute where we can. As the ecosystem evolves, so does GATA HUB.
