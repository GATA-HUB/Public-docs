# User Guide

### Before you start

* **You'll need** [**Keplr**](https://www.keplr.app/) (browser extension or mobile). One Keplr account works across Cosmos Hub, AtomOne, and Akash.
* **Keep your seed phrase private.** GATA will never DM you first or ask for it. Bookmark the official GATA HUB link and only use that.
* GATA HUB is **non-custodial** — you approve every action in Keplr. The app can't move your funds.

***

### 1. Connect your wallet

1. Open GATA HUB and click **Connect wallet**.
2. Approve the connection in Keplr.
3. That's it — GATA HUB derives your addresses on all chains and loads your delegations, NFTs, and Tier Score.

**First visit?** A short guided **tour** walks you through your dashboard boxes and the tier ladder. You can skip it anytime.

**Claim your Welcome bonus** — new members get a one-time Tier Score bonus. Look for the welcome banner on the dashboard and click **Claim**.

***

### 2. Read your dashboard

Your personal panel shows, at a glance:

* **Tier Score** — your total, and your current tier.
* **Delegation Score** — points from staking.
* **NFT Score** — points from holding/staking GATA NFTs.
* **Badges** — total score earned from badges.
* **Claimed** — your lifetime rewards claimed (in USD). **Click it** to open the Rewards popup:
  * **Claimed** — Raffles, Delegations (sats), NFTs, since 2022.
  * **Unclaimed** — what's ready to claim right now (pending raffle prizes, sats, PHOTON).

Click any box, or a tier circle on the progress bar, to see a full breakdown.

***

### 3. Delegate (and earn the Loyalty Bonus)

The **Delegate** tab lets you stake to the GATA validators on each chain.

1. Choose a chain and enter an amount (or tap **MAX**).
2. Click **Delegate** and approve in Keplr.
3. Already staked elsewhere? Use **Move to GATA** to redelegate to the GATA validator (no unbonding wait).

Staking does two things: it **raises your Delegation Score** (by token count, weighted per chain) and it starts accruing your **Loyalty Bonus** in Bitcoin.

> ⚠️ **Unbonding takes \~21 days** (the chain's standard period) during which tokens don't earn and can't be moved. Validators can be slashed for faults. Delegate with that in mind.

***

### 4. Manage NFTs (hold, stake, burn)

The **NFTs** tab shows your GATA collections.

* **Hold** yield collections to earn NFT Hold Score and a share of the monthly PHOTON pool.
* **Stake** your Genesis cats (Colonial / Voyager) — they only score and earn **while staked**.
* **Burn** an NFT to earn permanent Tier Score, valued at the collection's live floor price.

> 🔥 **Burning is permanent.** A burned NFT is destroyed forever. Genesis cats can't be burned. If a burn happens during a marketplace data outage, its points are credited automatically once pricing recovers — you don't lose anything.

***

### 5. Claim your rewards

Open the **Rewards** tab. You'll see two reward cards, each ticking up live:

* **NFT Reward** — your accruing **PHOTON** (paid on AtomOne).
* **Loyalty Bonus** — your accruing **Bitcoin/sats** (paid as allBTC on Osmosis, to your `osmo1…` address).

To claim: open a reward, click **Claim**, and sign in Keplr. The payout is sent to you and a transaction hash is recorded.

**Notes**

* Rewards **never expire** — let them accrue and claim when it's worth the fee.
* A small **minimum claim** applies to each reward so fees never exceed the payout.
* Sold or unstaked an NFT? Its earned PHOTON up to that point was **sealed** and is still claimable.

***

### 6. Enter raffles

The **Raffle** tab shows the current draws.

* **Weekly / Monthly / Surprise** — enter by activity (delegating, burning) or by buying tickets from the **Boost** tab during the draw.
* **Quarterly / Yearly** — your **tier** enters you automatically (higher tiers get more standing tickets).

Won something? Your win appears under **My Wins** — click **Claim** and sign to receive the prize (PHOTON, BTC, USDC, or an NFT).

**Verify any draw** — open the **verify** page for a raffle to re-run the exact draw in your browser using the public drand randomness. Winners you compute should match the recorded ones.

***

### 7. Boost your standing

The **Boost** tab lets you:

* **Buy raffle tickets** for the active draw.
* **Buy Tier Score** directly (spend counts toward your score).

Plus it shows your wallet balances across chains.

***

### 8. Climb the ladder

Grow your Tier Score to move up: **Fallen → Ember → Seeker → Devout → Radiant → Star.** Higher tiers unlock:

* A **bigger Loyalty Bonus boost** on your Bitcoin reward.
* **Standing tickets** in the quarterly/yearly raffles.
* **Tier badges** (each worth permanent Tier Score) and status.

Ways to earn Tier Score: stake to GATA validators, hold/stake GATA NFTs, burn NFTs, refer friends (**+500 each**, up to 5, when they qualify), complete milestones, and contribute to the community **Season** goal.

***

### Troubleshooting

* **My score looks stale after staking.** Scores refresh on sync; give it a moment after your transaction confirms, or reconnect. Background syncs also run automatically.
* **A page felt slow on first open.** First navigation can take a moment to load; after that it's instant.
* **My reward won't claim.** Check you're above the minimum-claim amount, and that your `osmo1…` (for BTC) or AtomOne (for PHOTON) path is set up in Keplr.
* **A raffle prize didn't arrive.** Prize payouts are token-exact and recorded with a tx hash; if a claim is interrupted it becomes claimable again shortly.

***

### Safety reminders

* GATA HUB **never** asks for your seed phrase, and the team will **never DM you first**.
* Only use the **official** GATA HUB link; verify contracts through official channels.
* **Burning is irreversible.** **Unbonding takes \~21 days.** Crypto is volatile — participate responsibly.
