# Caution

### ⚠️ Cautions — please read

**Not financial or investment advice.** GATA HUB is a community engagement and rewards dashboard. Nothing in it is a recommendation to buy, sell, or stake any asset, and Tier Score is **not** a security, share, or promise of profit.

**Rewards are not guaranteed returns.** The NFT (PHOTON) reward pool and the delegator (BTC/sats) bonus depend on **program funding, treasury balances, and on-chain conditions** (staking APR, commission, token prices). Reward rates and economic settings are **team-tunable** and can change. Reward figures shown are estimates that accrue over time, not fixed yields.

**Crypto assets carry risk.** ATOM, ATONE, AKT, PHOTON, BTC, NFTs and all tokens are volatile and can lose value. Only participate with what you can afford to risk.

**Staking has real mechanics.** Delegating locks tokens; **unbonding takes the chain's standard period (typically \~21 days)** during which tokens don't earn and can't be moved. Validators can be subject to **slashing** for downtime or faults. Understand these before delegating.

**Some actions are irreversible.** **Burning an NFT destroys it permanently.** Confirm carefully — burned NFTs cannot be recovered.

**You are your own custodian.** You sign every transaction in Keplr. Protect your seed phrase; never share it. The GATA team can never recover a lost wallet, and will **never DM you first** or ask for your seed phrase or private keys.

**Third-party dependencies.** GATA HUB relies on Keplr, the underlying blockchains, Osmosis (for BTC payouts), and marketplace data providers. Outages or changes on those systems can delay syncs, claims, or reward pricing.

**Minimum claim thresholds apply.** Small reward balances may not be claimable until they pass a minimum (so fees never exceed the payout). Let them accrue.

**Do your own research.** Verify contract addresses and links through official GATA channels before interacting. Beware of impersonators, fake "support," and lookalike sites.
