# FAQ

**Is GATA HUB custodial? Can it move my funds?** No. It's non-custodial. You sign every delegation, burn, purchase, and reward claim in Keplr. The reward payout wallet is operated separately by the team and only ever _sends_ rewards to you — it never has access to your wallet.

**Do I need three wallets for three chains?** No. One Keplr account works across all chains (same coin type). Connect once.

**Does my Tier Score drop if crypto prices fall?** No. Delegation Score is by **token count**, not dollar value. Only **unbonding** reduces it. NFT Hold Score drops only if you **sell** the NFT.

**Do rewards expire?** No. Both rewards accrue continuously and wait until you claim.

**Why is there a minimum claim amount?** So a claim never costs more in network fees than it pays out. Let small amounts accrue and claim later.

**What token is the Loyalty Bonus paid in?** Bitcoin — specifically **allBTC on Osmosis**, sent to your `osmo1…` address.

**What is PHOTON?** The native token of **AtomOne**. The NFT Holder Reward is paid in PHOTON on AtomOne.

**I sold / unstaked an NFT — did I lose its rewards?** No. What it accrued up to that moment is **sealed as a credit** and remains claimable.

**How do I know a raffle wasn't rigged?** Every draw uses public **drand** randomness committed _before_ the outcome is knowable, and the **/verify** page re-computes the winners in your own browser.

**What are the "47 GATA Epochs" in my Claimed panel?** Historical rewards from earlier GATA reward epochs, credited to your lifetime claimed total.

**Are the numbers (weights, pool size, boosts) fixed?** No — several economic dials are **team-tunable** and can be adjusted without a new release. The values shown in the app are always current.

**Is any of this investment advice or a guaranteed return?** No. See the Cautions section in the Product Summary. Rewards depend on program funding and on-chain conditions, and crypto assets carry risk.
